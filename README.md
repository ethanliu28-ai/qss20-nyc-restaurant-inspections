# qss20-nyc-restaurant-inspections

NYC Restaurant Inspection Disparities

This project asks whether NYC restaurant inspection outcomes vary systematically rather than purely reflecting restaurant-level food safety practices. I test whether violation scores differ significantly by location, cuisine type, year, or even by the restaurant name. Three sub-questions that probe potential drivers: (1) whether neighborhood income correlates with inspection outcomes, (2) whether ethnic cuisines show elevated critical violation rates for specific cuisines, and (3) whether high-tourist-traffic areas are inspected less frequently or score more favorably. Together these test whether NYC's inspection system operates as a neutral arbiter of food safety or reflects economic and geographic inequality across the five boroughs.
